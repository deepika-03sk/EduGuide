\# EduGuide 🎓



\### Your Guide to What Comes After +2



EduGuide is a web-based educational guidance platform designed to help students explore what they can pursue after completing +2/intermediate education.



It brings streams, courses, careers, entrance examinations, colleges, scholarships, and educational pathways together in one place.



\---



\## 📌 Overview



Choosing a career after +2 can be confusing because students need to consider multiple factors such as their stream, available courses, entrance exams, colleges, scholarships, skills, and career opportunities.



\*\*EduGuide\*\* provides a centralized platform where students can explore these options and understand possible educational pathways.



\---



\## ✨ Features



\### 1. Stream Explorer

Explore different +2 streams such as:



\- MPC

\- BiPC

\- MEC

\- CEC

\- Arts \& Humanities

\- Vocational



Students can view the fields and courses associated with each stream.



\### 2. Course Explorer

Explore undergraduate and diploma courses along with:



\- Course description

\- Duration

\- Eligibility

\- Related field



\### 3. Career Explorer

Explore different career opportunities and understand:



\- Required education

\- Required skills

\- Career opportunities

\- Job roles

\- Higher studies



\### 4. Entrance Exam Explorer

Provides information about entrance examinations including:



\- Exam name

\- Conducting body

\- Eligibility

\- Level

\- Purpose

\- Official website



\### 5. College Explorer

Explore colleges with information such as:



\- College name

\- Location

\- State

\- Type

\- Courses

\- Official website



\### 6. Scholarship Explorer

Explore scholarships and their:



\- Provider

\- Eligibility

\- Category

\- Amount

\- Description

\- Official website



\### 7. Education Pathway Explorer

Explore possible educational pathways after +2.



Pathway information includes:



\- Starting point

\- Destination

\- Relationship

\- Duration

\- Eligibility

\- Required skills

\- Career opportunities

\- Job roles

\- Higher studies

\- Entrance exams

\- Colleges

\- Future scope
